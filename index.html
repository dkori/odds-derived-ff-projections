Odds-Derived Fantasy Projections — Head-to-Head

Every player's fantasy-point outcome is a distribution reconstructed from sportsbook player-prop odds (de-vigged, then Monte-Carlo-simulated) — not a single number. Pick two players to compare their full ranges, with the market-implied mean/median and Yahoo's point projection side by side. Filter by position to narrow the search.
    Search and pick two players to compare head-to-head.

    1 · Probability of scoring at least X

    For any point total on the x-axis, the curve height is that player's chance of hitting that many points or more. Higher/further-right curve = better odds at that threshold. The dotted vertical line marks each player's median (the point total they clear about half the time). Good for "what's the chance they clear the ~12 I need from my flex?"

    2 · Difference (head-to-head)

    How many points the higher-projected player is ahead by at each percentile; where it dips below zero, the other player actually wins (e.g. a higher floor). Read a percentile as an outcome rank: the 10th is a bad game (floor), the 50th a typical game (median), the 90th a great game (ceiling). Hover any point to see each player's actual projected score there, so you can tell whether a 2-point edge is at 8 pts or at 25.

    3 · Density (shape only)

    The shape of each player's outcome distribution: taller = more likely, wider = less predictable. This illustrates the distributional differences well, but is less directly useful for roster decisions — the points where the curves cross aren't actionable. Charts 1–2 are the ones to reason from.

    Each player's chip shows our mean (maximize expected score) and our median (the "just hope they hit it" number), plus Yahoo's projection for reference, their position, and NFL team.
    Built from one static JSON (no server, no simulation at request time). Projections come from a single sportsbook's de-vigged player props, scored 0.5-PPR, correlated Monte-Carlo. Positions and Yahoo's point projection are joined from a public Yahoo players directory. Kickers and defenses are not modeled. See the project on GitHub for the methodology.
